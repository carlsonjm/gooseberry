# Roadmap

What is built in Gooseberry, what is still to come, and what is not planned.
Decisions still open are at the end, each named with what it holds up.

## Built

Milestones 0 to 4 are built, each with its checks by hand in `TESTING.md`:

- **Milestone 0, capture and keep.** The quick-note card, the board, tucking
  away and removing to the trash, and the note format written down
  (`FORMAT.md`).
- **Milestone 1, the planner.** Reminders at a time or the next time a document
  opens, the planner and checklists.
- **Milestone 2, folders.** Folders on the board and on disk, the quick note's
  Folder and Stuck to, a workspace's folder, and carrying a held note to a
  folder, a day or the trash.
- **Milestone 3, stuck to windows.** Gooseberry tells the desktop which windows
  have notes (`DESKTOP.md` § Stuck notes on the bus): Shuffle's title bar
  draws the dot, and Kadunce's Spread draws each card's stack.
- **Milestone 4, with Search.** Gooseberry answers `Find` and `OpenNote`, and
  Tettegouche lists notes among its results and hosts the quick note.

Gooseberry is ready for translation; its first translations come after 1.0.

## Still to come

- A note carried to the top edge opens Spread and is stuck to the card it is
  dropped on. It needs Kadunce to accept the carried note (`DESKTOP.md`
  § Needs).

## Later

- The notes tool for Split Rock: find, read and add, and next step from a
  note. On hold while Split Rock's own work is.

## Not planned

- Sync across computers, and a phone companion.
- The pen: handwritten notes, and handwriting read so ink is found.
- An account, a cloud service or a sync service of Gooseberry's own.
- Long documents and rich formatting. Gooseberry is for notes; a word processor
  is for documents.
- Shared or collaborative notes.

## Open decisions

None open. Settled ones are in `DECISIONS.md`; a new one is added here with two
options, a recommendation and what neither covers.
