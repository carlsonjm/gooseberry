# The mock-up

The approved visual direction (`../DECISIONS.md`). Six screens, each a 1280×800
tablet frame, kept as design source: open a file to read its exact layout,
sizes, colours and copy. They were drawn as live artboards, render only in the
canvas that drew them, and do not ship with the program.

| Screen | What it shows | Where it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Capture.dc.html` | Capture over a card: Type and Pen, colour, Checklist and Remind, Belongs to, Done, saved as written | Milestone 0, without Pen (not planned). A card the size of the search, laid out as `../DECISIONS.md` records, replaces the sheet from the bottom and its header; the Folder and Stuck to chips replace Belongs to. Checklist and Remind arrive in Milestone 1, under the note; `../DECISIONS.md` § The planner's controls records what they open |
| `Board.dc.html` | The board and the planner: places down the side, today's timed notes, undated ideas | Milestone 0; the planner column in Milestone 1 |
| `Search.dc.html` | Notes in Search, typed and handwritten | Milestone 4, typed notes only |
| `Genie.dc.html` | Next step from a note, with Split Rock | Later, on hold |
| `Spread.dc.html` | Notes on cards in Spread | Milestone 3, drawn by Kadunce |
| `Pinned.dc.html` | A rail of stickies beside the card | Superseded by the dot in the title bar and each card's stack in Spread (`../DECISIONS.md` § A window's notes are a dot in its title bar) |

The screens are drawn inside Shuffle, in Shuffle's appearance. On its own,
Gooseberry follows the desktop's theme. The mock-up sets the layout, sizes,
touch targets, note colours and behaviour. Sample note text is illustrative.
