# The mock-up

The approved visual direction (`../DECISIONS.md`). Six screens, each a 1280×800
tablet frame, kept as design source: open a file to read its exact layout,
sizes, colours and copy. They were drawn as live artboards and need the canvas
that drew them to render; they are not part of the program.

| Screen | What it shows | Where it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Capture.dc.html` | Capture over a card: Type and Pen, colour, Checklist and Remind, Belongs to, Done, saved as written | Milestone 0; the sheet from the bottom and its header are superseded by a card the size of the search, laid out as `../DECISIONS.md` records. Checklist and Remind in Milestone 1, under the note, with what they open as `../DECISIONS.md` § The planner's controls records. Type, Pen and the pen palette in Milestone 2, beside them, as § Writing by pen on a note records |
| `Board.dc.html` | The board and the planner: places down the side, today's timed notes, undated ideas | Milestone 0; the planner column in Milestone 1 |
| `Search.dc.html` | Notes in Search, typed and handwritten | Milestone 4; from Milestone 2 the board's own search shows a handwriting match as this does, with "Read as" |
| `Genie.dc.html` | Next step from a note, with Split Rock | Later |
| `Spread.dc.html` | Notes on cards in Spread | Later, once Kadunce offers it |
| `Pinned.dc.html` | A rail of stickies beside the card | Superseded: notes stack behind the card |

The screens are drawn inside Shuffle, in Shuffle's appearance. On its own,
Gooseberry follows the desktop's theme; the layout, sizes, touch targets, note
colours and behaviour are what the mock-up fixes. Sample note text is
illustrative.
