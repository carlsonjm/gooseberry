# Known limits

What Gooseberry does not do yet, and what it will not do.

## Not yet

- A note carried to the top edge does not open Spread or stick to the card it
  is dropped on. Kadunce does not accept a carried note (`DESKTOP.md`
  § Needs).
- Split Rock cannot use Gooseberry's notes yet: the notes tool it would call
  is not built (`DESKTOP.md` § Split Rock).
- Gooseberry's translations into German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese are
  machine-made, and native speakers have not checked them yet.

## Not planned

- Sync across computers, and a phone companion.
- The pen: handwritten notes, and handwriting read so ink is found.
- An account, a cloud service or a sync service of Gooseberry's own.
- Long documents and rich formatting. Gooseberry is for notes; a word processor
  is for documents.
- Shared or collaborative notes.
